# Third-party notices

## Predictive Technology Model

This project uses the University of Minnesota Predictive Technology Model
(PTM) for 45 nm bulk CMOS HP and LP devices. PTM model cards are third-party
materials and are intentionally **not redistributed** in this repository.
Download them from the official PTM page and review any terms supplied there:

- <https://mec.umn.edu/ptm>

The repository records expected filenames, model metadata, and SHA-256 hashes
only to help users confirm that the intended files are used. The repository's
MIT License does not grant rights to the PTM model cards.

## ngspice and Python packages

ngspice, NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, and their dependencies are separate
third-party projects with their own licenses. They are not bundled here;
dependency names and tested versions are recorded for environment recreation.

## Simulation outputs

The CSV files, figures, and metric tables are derived from nominal PTM model
simulations. They are provided for educational reproducibility and do not
represent measured silicon, foundry process data, device qualification, yield
improvement, or production results. No warranty of physical accuracy is made.
